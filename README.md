# DancePlan

DancePlan is the all-in-one organization and budgeting app built for dancers, dance families, and anyone managing the costs and commitments of dance.

Keep track of classes, schedules, expenses, tuition, costumes, competitions, and more — all in one simple place. DancePlan helps you stay organized, plan ahead, and better understand where your money goes throughout your dance journey.

Whether you are a dancer managing your own goals, a parent balancing multiple dance expenses, or a studio family trying to stay on top of everything, DancePlan makes planning easier.

## Features:
• Organize dance schedules and important dates  
• Track dance-related expenses and budgets  
• Plan ahead for tuition, events, costumes, and competitions  
• Keep your dance commitments in one convenient place  
• Make smarter decisions with a clearer view of your dance expenses  

Dance requires dedication, time, and planning. DancePlan helps you focus more on your passion and spend less time worrying about staying organized.

Start planning your dance journey today with DancePlan.
